Card Dispute Operations Analytics

Problem
Leaders had no single view of card disputes. Questions about volume, Reg E deadlines and losses meant manual data pulls, so answers came too late to act on.
Insight
The data existed but was split across claims, credits and chargebacks, and metrics were defined differently from report to report. No one could see where claims stalled or where money was lost.
Initiative
Built one pipeline and dashboard that follows every claim from intake to chargeback recovery, measures each step against Reg E deadlines and monthly targets, and defines every metric in a shared data dictionary.

Weekly Operations Performance Review

Problem
The weekly metrics meeting ran off a site of embedded dashboards and reports. The VP often wasn't sure what he was looking at, and presenters weren't sure what to call out.
Insight
The dashboards had the data but no story. Nothing said which metrics mattered, what to compare them against, or which changes were worth flagging.
Initiative
Built two Claude tools: an intake form where teams log each dashboard, its metrics and look-back windows, and a slide deck that refreshes before every meeting, finds what changed, and tells that story.
